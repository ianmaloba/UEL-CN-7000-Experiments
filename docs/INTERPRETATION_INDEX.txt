INTERPRETATION INDEX
====================

No dissertation findings exist in the initial scaffold.

When evidence is accepted, add one entry per result family:

RESULT_ID: <stable run or aggregate id>
FILES: <relative paths, space separated>
RESEARCH_QUESTION: RQ1 | RQ2 | RQ3 | RQ4
CLAIM: <one sentence limited to what the files support>
LIMITATIONS: <sample, proxy, model, evaluator, or uncertainty limits>
STATUS: pilot | accepted | rejected | superseded

Do not write an interpretation before the associated manifest, metrics, and quality
checks exist. A smoke test is labelled pilot and cannot be cited as a dissertation
finding.
